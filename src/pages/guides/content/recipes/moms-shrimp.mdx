Simple, quick, and very much a “yeet the soy sauce around the shrimp” kind of recipe.

## Ingredients

- Shrimp
- Ginger, cut into small pieces
- Vegetable oil
- Cooking wine
- Soy sauce
- Green onions, chopped

<Callout title="A recipe by feel">

The original notes don’t specify amounts or a serving size. Adjust the ginger, wine, soy sauce, and green onions to the amount of shrimp you’re cooking.

</Callout>

## Preparation

1. Clean the shrimp and drain well. Pat dry before they go into the hot oil.
2. Cut the ginger into small pieces and chop the green onions.

## Cooking

1. Warm the vegetable oil in a pan and add the ginger.
2. Add the shrimp after the oil.
3. Once the shrimp are a little cooked, add cooking wine.
4. Stir twice.
5. Add soy sauce — **yeet it around the shrimp** — and stir to coat.
6. Continue cooking. Mom’s notes say about **3 minutes**, watching for the shrimp to turn from gray to pink and become opaque.
7. Add the green onions and serve once the shrimp are cooked through.

<Callout title="Checking doneness">

The timing varies with shrimp size and pan heat. Health Canada recommends an internal temperature of **74°C / 165°F** for shrimp; colour alone isn’t a reliable safety check. [Safe cooking temperatures](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/safe-internal-cooking-temperatures.html).

</Callout>
